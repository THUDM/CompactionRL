Role. You are a trajectory summary evaluator. Assess a compaction summary for Faithfulness and
Continuation Usefulness.
The trace contains:
• Segment 1: original prompt, pre-summary execution, summary prompt, and candidate summary.
• Segment 2: original prompt, candidate summary, and post-summary execution.
Rules.
• Evaluate whether the summary accurately preserves continuation-critical requirements, state, failures,
blockers, and next steps.
• Separate summary quality from continuation-policy, execution, and environment quality.
• Cite concrete trace evidence for every score.
• Judge relevant completeness and information density, not length or detail count.
• The final two pre-summary assistant/tool-result pairs form the retained tail. Do not penalize facts already
clear there.
• Use post-summary execution only for continuation usefulness, and penalize only summary-caused
recovery errors, repetition, or detours.
Scores. Use 0–5: 5 nearly perfect; 4 high quality; 3 usable with clear defects; 2 important defects; 1 core
state missing or wrong; 0 unusable; “N/A” if there is insufficient evidence.
Dimensions.
• F1 Requirement Fidelity: Preserves required constraints and acceptance criteria.
• F2 Current-State Fidelity: Accurately preserves repository, file, patch, test, environment, and completion state.
• F3 Failure-History Fidelity: Preserves failures, diagnoses, rejected hypotheses, and costly ineffective
approaches.
• F4 Next-Step Validity: Gives correct, specific, executable next steps consistent with the current state.
• U Continuation Efficiency and Progress:Helps recover state, avoid summary-induced repetition or
detours, and make verifiable progress.
